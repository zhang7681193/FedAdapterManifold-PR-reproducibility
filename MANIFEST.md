## Archive structure

The archive contains:

- `configs/`: YAML experiment configurations for audited settings.
- `fedadaptermanifold/`: reusable implementation modules.
- `scripts/`: experiment, aggregation, plotting, audit, and packaging scripts.
- `tests/`: focused tests for reproducibility-critical utilities.
- `audit/`: fixed candidate pools, baseline fidelity, format, reference,
  no-oracle, and final-evidence validation records.
- `evidence/`: compact untouched-confirmation and training-only conditional
  `FAM|Geo` evidence used by the manuscript.
- `final_tables/`: recomputed manuscript-facing evidence summaries.
- `geometry_contract/`: frozen geometry registries and provider checks.
- `paper_resubmit/`: exact manuscript, supplement, and protocol snapshots used
  by consistency tests.
- `staging_clip_subspace_uncertainty_v2_20260813/`: the pooled confirmation
  analysis needed by the corresponding tests.

## Reproducibility notes

1. The package is intended to reproduce the reported diagnostics, tables, figures, and compact audit summaries.
2. Full reruns require local access to the relevant benchmark datasets and their licenses.
3. The package does not include raw PACS, OfficeHome, DomainNet, or MedMNIST images.
4. The manuscript reports audited five-seed summaries and paired client-seed statistics; compact result CSVs are included where possible.
5. Candidate pools and calibration splits are fixed before test evaluation;
   manifests and selection logs are included for audit.
6. The separately packaged frozen geometry payload is identified by registries
   and SHA256 hashes. External benchmark images are never redistributed.
7. A clean extraction of this archive reports 299 passed tests and four
   deliberate skips for optional PyTorch/upstream-source checks and historical
   raw result roots not redistributed in the compact archive.
